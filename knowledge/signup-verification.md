# Coldpilot — Account signup, verification, and login

## Creating an account

- Sign up with your **email + password**, or sign up with **Google**.
- On signup you get:
  - A **free account** with a **14-day trial**.
  - **1,000 signup credits** (granted after your email is verified).
- Billing currency is chosen from your country (₦ for Nigeria, $ elsewhere).

## Email verification (required)

- **Every new account must verify their email** before they can use the
  product.
- A verification email with a link is sent when you sign up.
- **If you don't verify and try to log in, Coldpilot re-sends a fresh
  verification link automatically** on every login attempt — so you never get
  stuck without a link.
- Google signups are also required to verify their email before the first
  login.
- Your 1,000 signup credits are only granted once the email is verified
  (prevents fake/throwaway inboxes from minting credits).

## Logging in

- Login with your email + password or with Google.
- If your account isn't verified yet, you'll be routed to the verify screen
  and a fresh verification link is sent to your inbox.

## Password reset

- Forgot your password? Use the **reset password** flow on the login page — a
  reset link is emailed to you.
- After a successful reset you can sign in with the new password.

## Security

- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
- Coldpilot has anti-abuse protections (device/IP fingerprinting, mailbox
  fingerprinting) that may flag genuinely suspicious signup patterns — the
  verification gate is part of this.

## Why do I have to verify?

- It confirms your email address actually belongs to you.
- It protects Coldpilot's sending reputation (spam prevention) and stops fake
  accounts from claiming signal credits.
- Once verified you keep full access; verification only needs to happen once.

## What happens after verifying

- Welcome email sent.
- Your signup credits are granted.
- You can complete onboarding (connect your first inbox, import leads, create
  your first campaign) and starting using the product.
