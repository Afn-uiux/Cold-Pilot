# Coldpilot — Plans, trial, pay-as-you-go, and plan expiry

## The plans

| Plan | Price (one-time per month) | Leads | Inboxes | AI writing |
|------|------|-------|---------|-----------|
| Free | ₦0 | 300 total leads | 2 inboxes | No |
| Starter | ₦28,500 | 5,000 leads | Unlimited | Yes |
| Pro | ₦73,500 | 30,000 leads | Unlimited | Yes |
| Agency | ₦148,500 | 150,000 leads | Unlimited | Yes |

- **Free** gives you a 14-day trial of the full experience (including AI) while
  the trial is active.
- **Starter / Pro / Agency** unlock AI writing and unlimited inboxes.
- Paid plans come with unlimited connected inboxes.

## Trial

- Every new free account gets a **14-day trial** the moment they sign up.
- While the trial is active, new free users get the paid features (AI included)
  and **1,000 signup credits** for free to try everything.
- When the trial ends, free users revert to the Free plan limits unless they
  upgrade or still have purchased credits left.

## Credits & pay-as-you-go

- Credits power every action: sending, verification, AI, and lead import.
- Every new account gets **1,000 free credits** on signup (after their email is
  verified).
- If a free user **buys credits**, they become **pay-as-you-go**: full feature
  access (unlimited leads, AI included) for as long as their balance is
  positive. Each action just costs credits.
- **Purchased credits never expire.** The free/signup bonus is spent first, and
  any leftover free portion is revoked when the trial ends; purchased credits
  always stay.

## Plan expiry

- Paid plans are purchased as a **one-time charge that lasts 30 days**, then
  expire back to the Free tier (this is not an automatic recurring card
  charge today).
- Coldpilot reminds you **3 days** and **1 day** before your plan ends.
- When it ends, your account drops back to Free, but **all your data and
  credits stay** — only plan-gated extras are revoked.
- To keep a plan, buy it again before it expires.

## Currency

- Billing currency is **Naira (₦)** for users in Nigeria and **US Dollars ($)**
  for everyone else, based on your country.
- You can switch your displayed currency between ₦ and $ in settings.
