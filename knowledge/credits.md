# ColdPilot — Credits

Credits are the metered unit that powers ColdPilot. Every paid action consumes
credits; your balance is shown in the dashboard.

## What costs credits

| Action | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Sending an email (campaign) | 0.02 credits |
| Verifying one lead | 0.25 credits |
| AI generation (write / spin / check / sequence) | 2 credits |
| Importing one lead | 0.05 credits |

## Getting credits

- **1,000 free credits** on every new account once their email is verified.
- **Credit packs** you can buy:
  - 100 credits — ₦7,500
  - 500 credits — ₦22,500
  - 2,000 credits — ₦60,000
  - 10,000 credits — ₦150,000
- Purchased credits never expire. The free signup bonus is always spent first.

## Pay-as-you-go

- A free user with purchased credits is **pay-as-you-go**: they keep full access
  (unlimited leads and AI) for as long as their balance stays positive. Each
  action just spends credits.
- When the free/signup portion of the balance is removed at trial end, any
  **purchased** credits remain and keep pay-as-you-go active.
- A user at zero balance is never locked out of reading their own data — only
  new consumption (sends, imports, AI) stops.

## When you run out

- Campaigns automatically **pause** when credits run out and **resume automatically**
  the moment you top up — no need to manually restart anything.
- You'll see a clear "insufficient credits" message on paid actions telling you
  what's needed and your current balance.

## Important behaviors

- Importing leads **permanently** counts toward your plan's lead limit, even if
  you later delete them — deleting is cleanup, not a limit reset.
- Deleting a lead does **not** refund its import or verification credits.
