The Problem

Most cold emails never get seen.

Over 80% of cold emails land in spam — not because the message is bad, but because the sender's domain isn't trusted by mailbox providers.

People send from unwarmed inboxes with no rotation, no sending limits, and no deliverability checks — so even great emails never reach the inbox.

When someone replies, the sender doesn't notice. They keep following up — and burn the relationship before it starts.

How AI Contributes

AI does the heavy lifting. You stay in control.

AI writes personalized email copy based on the details you provide about each lead — so you're not rewriting the same email for every person. It monitors inbox health before sending and flags anything that could hurt deliverability.

The human stays in control of the message and the strategy. AI handles the work that would take hours to do manually — personalization at scale, deliverability checks, and real-time reply detection that stops sequences the moment someone responds.
